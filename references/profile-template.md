# Profile template

Copy this to `~/job-hunt/profile.md` and fill it from the intake. Plain markdown
on purpose: the person can read it, edit it, and see exactly what the search is
working from.

Keep it under two pages. A profile that grows to eight pages stops getting read,
by the person and by the search.

---

```markdown
# Candidate profile

Last updated: YYYY-MM-DD

## Identity

One paragraph. The trajectory and the unusual intersection, not a title.

Example shape: "Seventeen years in client-facing operations at managed service
providers, support through team lead through quality operations, who now builds
and runs production AI systems. The signature is governance in code: release
gates, spend caps enforced at the router, evals that gate a weekly deploy."

## Target roles

In fit order. One line each on why it fits.

1. **Role type**: why this is the best fit.
2. **Role type**: why.
3. **Role type**: why.
4. **Role type**: the volume fallback, honestly labeled as such.

### Stop chasing

Role types to keep out of the shortlist, and one reason each.

- Role type: aims below the portfolio.
- Role type: a different trade.
- Role type: hard-gated on credentials not held.
- Role type: the person does not want this work.

## Location and authorization

- Lives in: city, country.
- Citizenship: country. Permits or visas held, and what each authorizes.
- Can invoice through: entity and country, or "no business entity".
- Timezone and workable overlap hours.
- Relocation: yes and where, or no.

**What this means for the search:**

- Fully open: list the categories.
- Longshot, pitch anyway: list them with the angle that makes them possible.
- Closed: list them so no run surfaces them again.

State any uncertainty here as uncertainty rather than resolving it by guess.

## Compensation

- **Floor: $X.** Below this, drop the role.
- Target: $Y. This ranks, it never filters.
- Employment, contract, or both. Note any strong preference.
- Hours available per week. Part-time or fractional: yes or no.
- Available from: date or notice period.

## Proof

### Numbers, single source of truth

Every number here is defensible and sourced. Nothing enters this table that the

### Systems and work shipped

### Public artifacts

## Limits

- Excluded companies and platforms, permanent unless noted.
- Work not taken.

## Cadence

- Hours per week for the search.

## Open questions

Things the profile cannot answer yet. Each run that hits one confirms it with
the person and moves the answer up into the profile.
```
