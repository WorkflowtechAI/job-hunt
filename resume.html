Resume

Renders your résumé markdown as a page, so you can print it to PDF. Everything stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Drop a résumé file here (resume/master.md, or a tailored copy), or:

The dashed lines are where the paper runs out. Entries are kept whole when printing, so a real break can land above a line and the page count can come out higher, never lower. In the print dialog, turn headers and footers off, because the footer prints your computer's file path into the PDF you send. Margins to default, scale 100%. Two pages is the ceiling for most people, one for early career.
